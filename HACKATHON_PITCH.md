# Pitch

Emergency operations centers often have data, but not a shared answer to “what should happen next?” RESCUECHAIN turns fragmented reports into a transparent decision-support loop: estimate who may be affected, separate that from what responders have verified, rank need, identify constrained resources, and let an accountable officer approve the plan.

The differentiator is the handoff from prediction to action without surrendering control. Gemini helps understand messy reports and explain recommendations; deterministic scoring and optimization own numerical decisions. Scenario planning lets officials test rainfall, closures, fleet, hospital, and shelter changes before reallocating scarce help.
