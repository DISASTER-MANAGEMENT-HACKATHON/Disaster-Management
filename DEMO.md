# Five-minute demo

1. Open the Command Center and point out `DEMO ENVIRONMENT`, system health, and estimated/verified counts.
2. Select `INC-0241` from the critical list. Explain the priority score and transparent factor breakdown.
3. Show the recommended boats, medical team, ambulance, and 18-minute estimate. Click **Review recommendation**, then explain that approval is recorded and does not auto-dispatch.
4. Click the map pins to show nearby resource markers and the incident map legend.
5. Click **Run scenario** in Scenario Planning. The changed critical count and affected population illustrate a planning what-if, not a claim about the future.
6. Close with the Resource Watch: shortage signals are forecasts from demo data and need an officer decision.
